SECTION 06

My Creative Workflow

A structured 6-stage methodology ensuring clarity, speed, and design excellence.

01
DISCOVER

Understand Goals

Understand the brand, audience, goals, core message, and specific project requirements.

02
RESEARCH

Analyze & Benchmark

Study competitors, market trends, typography references, and define the overall visual direction.

03
IDEATE

Concept & Moodboard

Generate creative concepts, visual sketches, moodboards, and explore multiple artistic directions.

04
DESIGN

Craft Visual Assets

Create the visual design using appropriate tools, typography, colors, images, and precise grid composition.

05
REFINE

Review & Polish

Review the design, collect feedback, fine-tune alignment, spacing, and polish tiny visual details.

06
DELIVER

Final Handout

Prepare final assets in required formats (vector SVG, high-res PNG, PDF, print-ready) and deliver.
